Is this parking space still reserved?

In Bratislava-Dúbravka, many parking spaces still have a white cross and a licence plate sign long after the rental has ended. This unofficial map uses your location to show which spaces nearby are rented according to the district's public records, and which markings are just leftovers from an old reservation.

Open the map

How it works

  1. Open the map on your phone and allow location access. It zooms in on your surroundings and loads all spaces within 200 m.
  2. If you don't share your location, or you're somewhere else right now, move the map to where you want to park and tap „Hľadať tu“ (Search here).
  3. Tap a space to see its record: type, car park, space number and when the record was last updated.

What the colours mean

  • Red – a reserved space with a registered tenant. Don't park here.
  • Yellow – a reserved space with no tenant on record.
  • Green – a public space. A white cross or licence plate sign here is a leftover from an old reservation.
  • Blue – a space for holders of a disabled parking card.

The sign wins, not the map

As long as a "reserved parking" traffic sign is standing, it is legally binding, even if the space has no tenant on record. Use the map as evidence when asking the district office to remove an outdated sign.

Where the data comes from

Every time you open it, the map loads the public parking space register of the Bratislava-Dúbravka district, the same data behind the district's official transport and parking map. The register covers Dúbravka only. Aerial photos: orthophoto 2026 © MČ Bratislava-Dúbravka.

Who made it
